What this means
Signal

Both skills have moved beyond demos into real customer hands — 17 distinct accounts, spanning enterprise marquees (Deloitte, Microsoft, Google, Stellantis, Qualcomm, CVS Health) and commercial adopters across five countries.

Momentum

Goal Insights is accelerating: June was its strongest month with more than double the prior monthly volume. Target Generation has the deeper base and the first three production go-lives (NETZSCH, Newfold, Stellantis).

Opportunity

Activity is concentrated in evaluation environments — Deloitte alone drives 52 runs without a production deployment. Converting these active evaluators into production is the clearest near-term growth lever.
